Logo Construction

The Lumina mark is built on a geometric grid using the golden ratio. Every curve and angle is mathematically derived for perfect optical balance.

  • Built on a 12×12 modular grid system
  • Optical corrections for small sizes
  • Clear space = height of "L"
  • Minimum size: 24px digital / 12mm print
  • Works on light, dark, and gradient backgrounds

10Usage Guidelines

Rules for maintaining brand consistency across all touchpoints.

✓
Do
  • Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "L" around the logo
  • Use the gradient version on dark or neutral backgrounds
  • Keep the mark square — never stretch or distort
  • Test contrast before placing the logo on photography
  • Scale proportionally when resizing
✕
Don't
  • Change the logo colors outside of the approved palette
  • Add effects like shadows, outlines, or bevels
  • Rotate the logo at odd angles
  • Place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Use the logo smaller than the minimum size
